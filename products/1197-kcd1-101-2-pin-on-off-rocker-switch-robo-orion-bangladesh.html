The KCD1-101 miniature rocker switch is the perfect combination of a high switching current and a highly compact design. Engineered for versatility, this 2-pin ON-OFF switch provides a robust power control solution for everything from 12V DIY Arduino projects to high-voltage 250V household appliances.

Encased in a rugged Nylon 66 housing, the switch is designed for a high mechanical life spanning 50,000 to 100,000 cycles. It features a convenient snap-in mounting mechanism, meaning you simply push it into a 17x13mm panel cut-out without the need for additional screws or mounting hardware. The button is clearly marked with 'I' and 'O' for intuitive operation.

Due to its industry-standard mounting dimensions and ability to handle heavy loads up to 10A, the KCD1-101 is widely used in automotive mods (cars, motorcycles, boats), water dispensers, extension sockets, and industrial machinery.

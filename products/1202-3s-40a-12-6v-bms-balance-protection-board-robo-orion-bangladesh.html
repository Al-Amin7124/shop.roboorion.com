When building high-power 12V lithium battery packs, standard protection boards simply aren't enough. The 3S 40A 12.6V 18650 Lithium Battery Balance BMS Protection Board is engineered specifically for heavy-duty applications. Powered by an array of 10 low-load resistance MOS tubes, this robust module can comfortably sustain a continuous discharge current of up to 40A, making it ideal for powering electric drills, high-drain motors, sprayers, and low-power inverters.

A standout feature of this "Balanced Version" is the integrated 100mA cell balancing circuit. This ensures that all three series-connected batteries (3.6V/3.7V nominal, 4.2V full) charge and discharge evenly, drastically improving the overall health and lifespan of your 18650 or 26650 battery pack.

Safety is paramount, and this board is fully equipped with overcharge, over-discharge, and overcurrent protections. It also features an AUTO Recovery function, meaning that if a fault condition occurs, the board will automatically restore power once the issue is resolved—no manual reset required. Please note: For continuous operation near the 40A upper limit, ensure your enclosure has adequate cooling.

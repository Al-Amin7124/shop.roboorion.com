The 5V DC 180 Motor is a highly versatile and incredibly fast compact motor, ideal for demanding small-scale DIY projects in Bangladesh. Designed to operate efficiently across a wide voltage range of 3.7V to 7.4V, it delivers immense rotational speeds required for high-velocity applications. At just 5V, it performs exceptionally well, but when pushed to its maximum 7.4V (like from a 2S Li-Po battery), it spins at an astonishing 36,000 RPM.

With a no-load current of 1A, this 180PH motor provides excellent torque for its size, allowing it to drive small propellers, geared wheel assemblies, and lightweight fans without stalling. Weighing only 32 grams, it will not bog down your flying or driving RC models with excess weight.

Due to its high RPM output, this motor is a favorite among hobbyists building custom RC cars, boats, foam-board airplanes, and small robotic chassis. To maximize its lifespan and efficiency, we recommend pairing it with proper motor drivers (like L298N or TB6612) and a strong power source.

How It Works

The DC Motor Speed Controller PWM 1203B regulates motor speed through pulse width modulation (PWM). By varying the duty cycle of the electrical pulse, it controls the average voltage supplied to the motor terminal, allowing for smooth, noise-free, and efficient speed adjustments. The built-in safety circuitry continually monitors power output, ensuring the motor stays protected from sudden spikes or thermal issues.

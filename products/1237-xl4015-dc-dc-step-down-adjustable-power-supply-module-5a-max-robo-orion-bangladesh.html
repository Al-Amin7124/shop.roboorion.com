The XL4015 5A DC-DC Step Down Adjustable Power Supply Buck Module is a highly efficient 180 KHz fixed frequency PWM buck (step-down) DC/DC converter. It is designed to reliably drive a 5A load while offering outstanding line and load regulation. With an efficiency rating of up to 96% and low ripple output, this module is an essential component for projects requiring high-power voltage reduction.

This module accepts a wide input voltage range of 4V to 38V DC and steps it down to a finely adjustable output voltage ranging from 1.25V to 36V DC. It is robustly built with multiple internal protection mechanisms, including thermal shutdown, current limiting, and output short-circuit protection, safeguarding both the module and your connected electronics.

Perfect for applications where the primary power source is significantly higher than the required operating voltage, the XL4015 is frequently used in custom battery chargers, DIY regulated power supplies, 24V vehicle laptop adapters, telecom equipment, and industrial step-down applications.

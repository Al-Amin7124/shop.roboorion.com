The TEC1-12706 Thermoelectric Cooler (Peltier Module) is an incredibly fascinating solid-state active heat pump. When you supply standard 12V DC power to the module, it immediately begins transferring heat from one side of the ceramic plate to the other. Within seconds, one side becomes freezing cold (capable of forming ice under the right conditions), while the opposite side becomes extremely hot.

Due to its reliable solid-state nature (no moving parts or refrigerants), it is incredibly popular for DIY projects such as custom mini-fridges, beverage coolers/warmers, portable camping coolers, and experimental CPU/water cooling loops.

⚠️ CRITICAL USAGE WARNING

You MUST attach a large aluminum heatsink and a cooling fan to the HOT side of this module before powering it on. Thermal paste is required. If the hot side is not actively cooled, the module will overheat and permanently destroy itself within 3 to 5 seconds. Reversing the polarity will reverse which side gets hot/cold.

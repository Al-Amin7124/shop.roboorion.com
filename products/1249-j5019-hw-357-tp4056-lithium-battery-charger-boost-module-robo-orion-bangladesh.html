The J5019 HW-357 TP4056 UPS Charger Board is an incredibly practical, all-in-one power management solution designed for compact DIY electronics. It integrates a proven TP4056 lithium battery charging circuit with a highly efficient DC-DC step-up (boost) converter onto a single PCB. This clever design enables you to charge a 3.7V battery (like an 18650) while simultaneously powering a connected load, making it functionally equivalent to a miniature Uninterruptible Power Supply (UPS).

The integrated boost converter features a trim potentiometer, allowing you to easily adjust the output voltage anywhere from 4.3V up to 27V. Whether you need 5V for an Arduino, 9V for an instrument, or 12V for a WiFi router, this module handles it with up to 93% conversion efficiency. It accepts a standard 4.5V to 8V DC input, making it fully compatible with USB sources, small solar panels, and generic power bricks.

Its combination of small size (33x23x10 mm), simple solder-pad wiring, and versatile output range makes the HW-357 an excellent choice for students, engineers, and hobbyists building portable enclosures, battery backup systems, and embedded IoT devices.

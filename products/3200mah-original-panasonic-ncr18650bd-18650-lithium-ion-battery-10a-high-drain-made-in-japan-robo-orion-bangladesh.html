Safety Notes

  1. Please fully charge before first use.
  2. Use only high-quality chargers specifically designed for lithium-ion batteries.
  3. A lithium-ion battery should NEVER be discharged below 2.5V.
  4. NEVER exceed the battery's maximum continuous discharge rating.
  5. DO NOT short-circuit the battery — keep away from loose change and metal objects.
  6. DO NOT use if there is abnormal smell, heat, deformities, or discoloration.
  7. DO NOT crush, hit, or disassemble the battery.
  8. DO NOT dispose of in fire or near high temperatures (above 60°C).
  9. DO NOT expose to water, seawater, or beverages.
  10. If electrolyte contacts eyes, wash immediately with clean water and seek medical treatment.

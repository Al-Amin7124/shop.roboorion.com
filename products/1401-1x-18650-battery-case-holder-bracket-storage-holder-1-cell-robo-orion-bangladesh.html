Integrating 3.7V lithium-ion power into your projects has never been cleaner. The 1X 18650 Battery Case Holder Bracket provides a neat, professional storage solution for a single standard 18650 battery cell. This generic battery storage box is molded from tough ABS plastic, ensuring excellent durability and resistance to accidental drops.

What sets this specific model apart is its connection style. Instead of standard floppy wire leads, this holder is equipped with rigid, conductive bottom pins. These hard pins are perfectly designed to drop directly into through-holes on a custom Printed Circuit Board (PCB), allowing for fast, secure, and flush soldering.

Whether you are building a custom power bank, powering an ESP32 IoT node, or constructing a 3.7V robotic chassis, this 1-cell holder provides a stable mechanical hold and excellent electrical connection for your high-discharge 18650 cells.

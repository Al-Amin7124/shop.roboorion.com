The Mini Router UPS Board 42W V4 by RC Product BD is the latest high-performance power backup module tailored for maintaining uninterrupted internet connectivity. Upgraded to handle up to 42W of combined output, this V4 board is the definitive solution to prevent your WiFi router or ONU from resetting when main power lines switch or fail.

With an improved, purely SMD layout, the board ensures extreme reliability and thermal efficiency. It accommodates multiple devices simultaneously by offering distinct 12V, 9V, and 5V outputs.

The 12V 4 Inch Brushless DC Cooling Fan is an incredibly versatile exhaust and intake fan designed for high-performance cooling. Measuring 92x92x25mm (commonly classified as a 4-inch fan), it strikes the perfect balance between robust airflow and compact installation. It is a highly sought-after component for makers and DIY enthusiasts looking to ventilate confined spaces effectively.

What sets this fan apart is its premium double-row ball bearing system. Unlike cheaper sleeve bearing fans that wear out quickly, the double ball bearing ensures long-lasting durability, smooth running, and remarkably low noise output even when running continuously at its 0.6A current rating. This makes it particularly ideal for noise-sensitive applications like egg incubators, home theater electronics cabinets, and desktop computer cases.

Installation is straightforward thanks to the pre-drilled 4mm mounting holes spaced exactly 70mm apart. The fan includes a standard 2-pin connector attached to a 150mm wire, making it plug-and-play for most power supplies and custom control boards.

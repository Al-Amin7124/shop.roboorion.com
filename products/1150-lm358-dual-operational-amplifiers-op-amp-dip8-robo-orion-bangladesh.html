The LM358 Dual Operational Amplifier is a highly versatile integrated circuit (IC) featuring two independent, high-gain operational amplifiers. Designed to operate from a single power supply over a wide range of voltages, it is an essential component for any electronics enthusiast or engineer.

One of the key advantages of the LM358 is its ability to handle a common-mode input voltage range that includes ground, allowing it to sense signals directly near the ground reference without needing a negative supply. It also features internal frequency compensation, reducing the need for external capacitors to stabilize the circuit.

Whether you are building active filters, signal conditioners, or simple comparators, the LM358 provides stable and reliable performance. Its low supply-current drain makes it suitable for battery-powered devices and portable instrumentation.

The BLX-A PCB Mount Fuse Holder provides a secure, reliable, and convenient method for integrating fuses directly onto printed circuit boards. It is engineered to seamlessly hold standard 5x20mm glass tube or ceramic fuses, ensuring a solid mechanical grip and stable electrical connection. This helps protect sensitive circuitry from overcurrent damage.

Constructed using high-quality thermoplastic and flame-retardant materials, the BLX-A guarantees durability under high-stress conditions. Rated to handle up to 15A at 125V AC, it easily covers a broad range of applications—from compact consumer electronics and DIY audio projects to complex power supply boards and industrial equipment.

Its space-saving form factor is ideal for dense PCB layouts, and its through-hole solder pins make installation straightforward using standard hand-soldering techniques.

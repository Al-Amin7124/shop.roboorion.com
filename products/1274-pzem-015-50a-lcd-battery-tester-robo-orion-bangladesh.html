Take complete control over your power management with the Peacefair PZEM-015 DC Battery Tester. This professional-grade, multi-functional LCD panel meter is designed to give you a deep, real-time understanding of your battery's health and performance. Unlike simple voltmeters, the PZEM-015 tracks 9 different electrical parameters simultaneously, making it an indispensable tool for solar setups, RVs, e-bikes, and DIY battery bank projects.

Supplied with a robust 50A external shunt, this meter safely measures currents up to 50 Amps and voltages up to a massive 200V DC. What truly sets this meter apart is its ability to measure Internal Resistance (0-999mΩ) and accumulated Capacity (AH). By tracking internal resistance, you can quickly identify degrading cells before they fail. The intuitive visual battery symbol (SOC) gives you an immediate percentage reading of the remaining charge.

Installation is straightforward, and the large, backlit LCD screen ensures data is readable even in dark environments. For testing standard batteries (8V to 200V), the meter powers itself directly from the test circuit. For testing ultra-low voltage cells (0V to 8V), it features a dedicated terminal for an external 3.3V-12V power supply.

Wiring and Application Notes

  • Independent Power Supply: If measuring voltages between 0V-8V, you must connect an independent 3.3V-12V DC power source to the auxiliary power pins.
  • Shunt Wiring: The shunt must be connected in series with the negative (-) line of the circuit. Do not connect it to the positive line.
  • Applications: Perfect for solar power walls, RV/marine auxiliary batteries, testing e-bike battery capacity, and monitoring DIY UPS power banks.

If you are looking to build professional-grade lithium battery packs, the K7-3 Farad Capacitor Double Pulse Spot Welder DIY Kit is the ultimate tool. Unlike cheaper LiPo battery-powered welders that degrade over time and suffer from internal resistance, this unit uses super farad capacitors to store and unleash incredibly high bursts of energy. This ensures a permanent, firm weld without blowing holes in your nickel strips or damaging the battery cells.

The K7-3 intelligent control board acts as the brain of the machine, featuring an easy-to-read LCD screen display that lets you accurately tune your double pulse settings. It connects seamlessly to thick 12 square millimeter (approx. 12 AWG) pure copper welding pens, delivering maximum current flow straight to the welding point with very low resistance. Whether you are assembling 18650, 21700, or 32650 battery packs, this butt-welding setup handles 0.1mm to 0.3mm nickel seamlessly.

Powering the unit is incredibly convenient thanks to the onboard Type-C port, which supports standard 5V 2A chargers. The capacitors charge up quickly, and the double pulse functionality ensures the first pulse clears the oxide layer on the nickel, while the second pulse delivers the deep weld. It is the perfect DIY machine for hobbyists, e-bike builders, and repair technicians.

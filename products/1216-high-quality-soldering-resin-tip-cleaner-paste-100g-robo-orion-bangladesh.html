The Golden Rooster (Jin Ji) Soldering Paste is a highly reliable, 100g tin of rosin-based flux designed to make your electronics assembly and repair work smoother. Widely favored in the electronics hobbyist world, this flux features a semi-solid, jelly-like consistency that is perfect for general PCB repair, wire tinning, and DIY projects.

Its primary active ingredient is high-quality rosin blended with chemical activators. When heat is applied via your soldering iron, the flux boils away, aggressively removing oxidation from the metal surfaces. This critical action improves the "wetting" of the solder, allowing it to flow seamlessly over joints to create strong, durable electrical and mechanical bonds.

With a neutral pH value (7 ±0.3), this paste is non-corrosive to most components at room temperature. To use, simply clean your iron's tip, apply a tiny amount of the paste to the pad or wire using a toothpick or small brush, and apply heat. While often marketed as "no-clean," it is best practice to clean the sticky residue left behind with Isopropyl Alcohol (IPA) to maintain the long-term integrity of your circuits.

When the power goes out, stay connected seamlessly with the J5019 Type-C Booster Module. This incredibly compact 2-in-1 circuit board serves as both a smart lithium battery charger and a highly efficient DC-DC step-up (boost) converter. It is the absolute standard for hobbyists and professionals looking to build low-cost, reliable DIY mini UPS systems for Wi-Fi routers and ONUs.

Using the board is simple. Power it via the modern USB Type-C port with a standard 5V adapter. It will safely charge a connected 3.7V Lithium-ion or Li-Po battery (such as an 18650 cell) while simultaneously powering your load. During a power outage, the module instantly pulls power from the battery, stepping up the 3.7V voltage to a stable output using the onboard potentiometer.

The adjustable output voltage gives you ultimate flexibility. With a small screwdriver, you can dial the output to exactly 5V, 9V, or 12V (or anywhere up to 27V) to perfectly match your router's power requirements. The module also features built-in over-charge protection [verify] to keep your lithium cells safe during continuous plugged-in use.

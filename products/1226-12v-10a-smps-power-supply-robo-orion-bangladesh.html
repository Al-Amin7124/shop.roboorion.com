The 12V 10A DC Switching Mode Power Supply (SMPS) is an industrial-grade power delivery unit designed to convert standard AC mains electricity into a highly stable 12V DC output. Capable of pushing up to 10A of continuous current, this module provides an impressive 120W of total power. It is highly trusted by electricians and DIYers for driving demanding loads such as long 12V LED strips, CCTV camera grids, 3D printers, and industrial automation equipment.

Designed for durability and safety, this SMPS features a robust metal case with an aluminum base, ensuring excellent passive heat dissipation without the noise of an internal fan. The internal circuitry is CE, FCC, CCC, and RoHs compliant, and comes fully equipped with modern safety features including short circuit, overload, and over-voltage protection.

Please Note: This power supply is designed for professional indoor installation. It does not come with installation instructions, AC wiring cables, or an on/off switch. Ensure you consult a qualified electrician if you are unfamiliar with wiring AC mains to screw terminals.

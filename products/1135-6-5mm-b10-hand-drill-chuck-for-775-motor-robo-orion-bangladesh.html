The 6.5mm B10 Hand Drill Chuck is a specialized clamping accessory designed to securely hold tools with radial symmetry, such as drill bits or small cylinders. Engineered seamlessly for compatibility with the highly popular DC 12V-24V 775 motor, this chuck is an essential component for any hobbyist building custom electric drills, mini mills, or lathes for precise mechanical operations.

Manufactured from high-carbon and hardened bearing steel, the D775 drill chuck set guarantees exceptional durability and an incredibly strong gripping force. It comfortably supports a wide universal bit size range from 0.6mm up to 6.5mm. This makes it a highly versatile choice for machining operations on materials like ABS boards, aluminum sheets, craft wood, and thick paperboards.

The Type-C BLDC Fan Driver Circuit Board with 4-Speed & Stepless Speed Control (3.7V to 12V Boost Module) is an essential replacement and DIY circuit board engineered specifically for modern rechargeable handheld, desk, and portable cooling fans. Featuring an integrated step-up boost converter, this module takes standard 3.7V single-cell lithium-ion or Li-Po battery power (such as 18650 cells) and scales output voltages dynamically across four distinct tiers: 5V, 7V, 9V, and up to 12V.

Built for versatility, the board features both a 4-speed preset switching mechanism and a smooth stepless speed regulation control. Operating status is clearly indicated by onboard LEDs (Red for 5V, Green for 7V, Blue for 9V, and multi-stage scaling for 12V). Integrated with a modern USB Type-C port, the circuit supports convenient battery recharging via standard mobile chargers or power banks, while drawing an ultra-low standby current (~10µA) to preserve maximum battery reserve.

Designed with reliable battery protection protocols (including reverse polarity, overcharge, and over-discharge prevention) alongside a beginner-friendly PH2.0 motor connector, this compact FR4 circuit board is the ideal choice for repairing broken handheld mini fans or fabricating custom wireless cooling gadgets for travel, office desks, or emergency backup during power cuts in Bangladesh.

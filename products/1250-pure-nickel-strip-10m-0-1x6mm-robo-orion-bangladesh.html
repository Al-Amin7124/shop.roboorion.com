Frequently Asked Questions

This product is guaranteed to be ≥99.7% Pure Nickel. Unlike cheap nickel-plated steel, pure nickel will not easily rust, offers much lower electrical resistance, and runs significantly cooler under high-current loads.

The 0.1mm thickness by 6mm width is the industry standard for 18650 battery cells. It easily fits on the battery terminals without shorting against the edges and can be effortlessly welded by standard DIY and professional spot welders.

You can perform a scratch and saltwater test; pure nickel will resist rusting far longer than steel. Alternatively, during grinding or spot welding, steel produces branching sparks, whereas pure nickel produces very few, short, straight sparks. Note that pure nickel is slightly magnetic, so a magnet test alone is not 100% conclusive.

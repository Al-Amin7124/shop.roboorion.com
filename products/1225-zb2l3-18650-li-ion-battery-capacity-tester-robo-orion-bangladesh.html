When searching for the best ZB2L3 battery capacity tester price in Bangladesh, you need a tool that provides accuracy without complexity. The ZB2L3 is an industrial-grade discharge tester specifically engineered to verify the actual Amp-hour (Ah) capacity of 18650 Li-ion cells and other rechargeable batteries.

Unlike basic voltage meters that only show surface charge, the ZB2L3 actively discharges the battery through a controlled load (using the included resistors). By monitoring the current and voltage over time, it calculates the precise energy remaining in the cell. This makes it an indispensable tool for DIY power bank builders, drone hobbyists, and technicians salvaging cells from laptop batteries.

Designed for safety and precision, the module features an automatic cutoff. Once the battery reaches the pre-set termination voltage, the module disconnects the load instantly, preventing the cell from over-discharging and suffering permanent damage.

Operating Instructions

  1. Fully charge the battery you intend to test.
  2. Connect battery to the center terminals (observe polarity).
  3. Connect load resistors to the outer terminals.
  4. Connect Micro USB power (5V charger). The display will show the current battery voltage.
  5. Press "OK" for auto-termination or use "+" / "-" to set a custom cut-off voltage, then press "OK".
  6. Once complete, the display blinks the final capacity in Ah. Press "OK" to reset.

Error Code Reference

  • Err1: Battery voltage exceeds 15V (Over-voltage).
  • Err2: Battery voltage is already below termination voltage.
  • Err3: Voltage drops too rapidly under load (High internal resistance).
  • Err4: Discharge current exceeds the 3.1A safety limit.

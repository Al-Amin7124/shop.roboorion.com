When building projects that strictly demand a 12V supply—such as DIY router UPS systems, CCTV backups, or 12V LED lighting—the Mini 12V Buck Boost Converter price in Bangladesh is exactly what makers look for. This incredibly tiny DC-DC module accepts any fluctuating input voltage from 2V up to 15V and locks the output securely at 12V.

Thanks to its automatic buck-boost design, it can act as a step-up (boost) converter when powered by a 3.7V 18650 battery or a 5V USB source, or it can act as a step-down (buck) converter if you connect it to a fluctuating 14V solar panel or an unstable automotive battery. The transition is seamless, ensuring your 12V electronics never drop connection or reset.

Despite its miniature size (approx 14x22mm), it delivers high efficiency up to 95% with low output ripple, making it perfect to embed inside 3D printed cases or tight enclosures alongside your router or ONU.

Note: While rated for 3A peak, continuous output current will be lower when boosting from a very low voltage like 3.7V to 12V due to input current limits and heat generation. Ensure adequate airflow if running near limits.

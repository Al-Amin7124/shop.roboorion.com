The DC Power Socket Female Panel Mount (5.5x2.1mm) is a highly reliable connector designed to provide a secure and clean power interface for custom electronic enclosures and DIY projects. Engineered to perfectly match standard 5.5mm x 2.1mm male barrel plugs, this socket features a threaded barrel design and includes a mounting nut, allowing it to be easily and rigidly mounted into a 12mm panel hole.

It is rated for a continuous electrical load of 12V at 3A, making it highly suitable for powering custom Arduino project boxes, 12V LED lighting controllers, amplifier enclosures, and compact robotics chassis. The rear solder terminals provide a strong, permanent connection point for your internal wiring, ensuring that your circuits remain powered without interruption.

Constructed from durable plastic and metal materials with excellent insulation resistance, this female DC socket ensures a stable, professional-grade power connection that won't easily disconnect under vibration or movement.

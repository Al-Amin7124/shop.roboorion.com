If you are looking for a compact, reliable self-locking button, this Black Latching Push Button Switch is the perfect choice. This mini-sized switch is heavily utilized to send manual control signals to relays, contactors, and electromagnetic starters. It is most commonly found as the main power button in standard rechargeable flashlights, portable lamps, and various household electrical appliances.

Featuring a classic self-locking (latching) mechanism, pressing the button once engages the circuit (ON), and pressing it again releases it (OFF). The switch is built with an insulated black plastic shell to protect against electrical leakage, ensuring safe operation. Meanwhile, the contact tips are made from high-quality copper, ensuring excellent conductivity and long-lasting durability when integrated into your circuit.

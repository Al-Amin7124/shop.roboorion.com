The 5V 2.4A Type-C USB Lithium Battery Charging with Protection Power Bank Circuit Board (IP5306) is a cutting-edge, ultra-compact synchronous step-up switching regulator and charger module designed to drive custom portable battery banks and DIY electrical solutions. Based on the highly integrated Injoinic IP5306 silicon design, this single-board system handles multi-stage battery charging and boost regulation inside one compact footprint, drastically simplifying bill-of-materials cost.

Fitted with a modern USB Type-C port, the circuit supports rapid input recharge rates up to 2.1A to quickly top up connected single-cell 3.7V Lithium-ion or Li-Po battery configurations (including flat-top 18650, 21700, and standard pouch lithium packs). On the discharge side, the high-efficiency step-up synchronous regulator converts internal cell energy into a stable 5.0V output, supplying up to 2.4A of output current through its integrated USB-A terminal to rapidly charge smartphones, tablets, or microcontrollers like Arduino and ESP32.

To prevent battery degradation and dangerous thermal runaway states, the board includes a full battery protection chipset layout. Integrated sensors trigger automatic output cutoffs when encountering short-circuits, output overvoltage, thermal over-temperature thresholds, or when the cell discharges below 3.0V. An intuitive onboard 4-level LED status array provides clear capacity tracking (25%, 50%, 75%, and 100%), making this reliable converter board perfect for DIY electronic enclosures, emergency portable power backup systems, solar-battery conversions, and low-cost field instrumentation power modules.

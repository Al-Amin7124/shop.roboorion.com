The Original Amass XT90 High Current Connector Male & Female Pair is a high-reliability, heavy-duty power coupler designed to manage high-amperage current flows safely. Built by Amass, the globally recognized manufacturer of high-quality RC and battery connectors, this pair is highly sought after by drone builders, RC hobbyists, and custom electric vehicle (EV) developers in Bangladesh.

Each connector pair features solid copper brass pins plated with a 2U thickness of pure gold. This creates an extremely low contact resistance (~0.30 mΩ), minimizing energy loss and preventing overheating under massive current loads. This module is fully rated to handle 90A of continuous operational current and up to 120A instantaneous peaks. The outer yellow casing is molded from high-grade, heat-resistant Nylon rated at UL94 V0, ensuring the terminals will not melt or deform during difficult high-AWG wire soldering.

Perfect for heavy-lift multirotor drones, large scale RC airplanes, custom e-bike battery packs, high-torque robotics, and solar array storage lines, this robust connector safely accommodates thick high-strand 8 AWG and 10 AWG silicone wires, ensuring consistent power delivery for over 1000 mating cycles.

The AC-DC 5V 700mA 3.5W Precision Buck Converter is a highly efficient step-down power supply module designed to convert standard 220V AC mains voltage into a stable 5V DC output. With a compact form factor, this module is an ideal power source for any DIY electronics project that requires a constant 5V supply.

Engineered for reliability, the module features low ripple and low noise output, ensuring that sensitive components like microcontrollers and sensors operate without interference. It also includes integrated overload and short-circuit protection to prevent damage to both the module and the connected circuitry.

This power supply is designed to meet strict EMC and Safety Tests, complying with UL and CE requirements. Whether you are building a smart home device, an Arduino-based project, or a custom electronic gadget, this module provides a high power density solution with minimal power consumption and environmental impact.

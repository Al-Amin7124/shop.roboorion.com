Ensure the safety and longevity of your DIY battery packs with the 3S 10A 12.6V 18650 Lithium Battery Balance BMS Protection Board. Designed for 3-serial-cell lithium-ion or lithium polymer rechargeable batteries, this module features a highly accurate voltage detector and an intelligent delay circuit. It is perfectly suited for nominal 3.7V cells that reach 4.2V at full charge, accommodating sizes like 18650, 26650, and flat Li-Po packs without any size limits.

Safety is paramount when working with lithium batteries. This BMS provides comprehensive protection functions including overcharge, over-discharge, short circuit, and overcurrent protection. With a maximum continuous discharge current of 10A and a transient limit of up to 10A, it effortlessly powers 12V consumer electronics, mini UPS systems, and custom chargers.

Its ultra-compact 50x16x1mm form factor makes it incredibly easy to tuck inside tight battery wraps or small project enclosures. Furthermore, the module automatically cancels the protection state once fault conditions are removed, ensuring seamless operation without manual resets.

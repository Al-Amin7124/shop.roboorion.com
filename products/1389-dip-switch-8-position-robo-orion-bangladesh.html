The 8 Position DIP Switch is a highly practical manual switch module designed for electronics hardware configuration. It contains 8 individual slide-style toggle switches arranged in a single compact block. These switches are widely used by engineers and hobbyists to set device addresses, configure logic states, or select operating modes directly on a PCB without needing a software interface.

Built with a standard 2.54mm (0.1-inch) pin pitch, this DIP switch is 100% compatible with standard solderless breadboards. You can plug it directly across the center dividing channel of a breadboard to manage 8 different digital logic signals effortlessly. It is an essential component for digital electronics labs, microcontroller projects, and testing logic gates.

The switches offer a satisfying, tactile "click" when sliding between ON and OFF positions. Rated for standard low-voltage DC signals (typically used with 5V or 3.3V logic), they provide reliable contact and a long mechanical life for prototyping or permanent circuit installation.

If you are searching for an affordable 3S 12V 10A BMS price in Bangladesh, this compact Battery Management System (BMS) is the perfect solution. Designed to protect and extend the life of 18650 lithium-ion battery packs, it is a crucial component for any 3-cell (3S) series configuration. This board ensures that your custom DIY battery packs operate safely under standard 11.1V nominal and 12.6V peak loads.

Safety is paramount when working with lithium batteries. This BMS provides comprehensive coverage, including an overcharge cutoff at approximately 4.25V per cell and an over-discharge cutoff at 2.5V. Furthermore, it incorporates highly responsive overcurrent and short-circuit protection, immediately halting power delivery if a fault occurs, thereby preventing battery fires or severe degradation.

What makes this particular module excellent for DIY enthusiasts is its built-in balance function. It ensures uniform charging across all three cells, guaranteeing that no single cell is over-stressed. Supporting a continuous discharge of 10A (and peaking up to 12A), this lightweight PCB is perfect for small e-bikes, LED lighting arrays, solar storage, and reliable DIY power banks.

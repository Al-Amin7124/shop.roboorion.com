The TDA2050 High Quality Audio Amplifier IC is a monolithic integrated circuit designed for professional-grade audio power amplification. Known for its exceptional fidelity and efficiency, the TDA2050 is widely used in home audio systems, active speakers, and custom Hi-Fi amplifier projects.

This IC is engineered to deliver an output power of up to 32W with very low harmonic distortion, ensuring a crisp and clear sound profile. It features a simplified circuit design that allows hobbyists and engineers to create high-quality amplifiers with minimal external components.

To ensure long-term stability and safety, the TDA2050 includes integrated thermal shutdown protection and short-circuit protection. These features prevent the IC from overheating or failing during unexpected electrical faults, making it a reliable choice for any audio application.

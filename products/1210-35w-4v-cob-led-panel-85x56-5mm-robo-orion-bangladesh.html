The KS8556-108-9 is a highly versatile and powerful 35W COB LED Panel designed for extreme brightness in DIY electronics and replacement applications. Packed with an astonishing 108 LED segments, this light board provides massive illumination perfect for outdoor lanterns, emergency light boxes, and custom solar lighting setups.

What makes this panel unique is its wide operating voltage range. While it can safely turn on and run efficiently at 4V, driving it at 9V unlocks its true potential. The board boasts dimensions of 85mm x 56.5mm while remaining incredibly slim with a thickness of just 0.65mm, allowing for versatile mounting in tight enclosures.

Lab Tested Results & 12V Usage Warning

  • At 4.0V: It draws ~1A, outputting around 5 Watts. (Safe for direct 18650 battery use).
  • At 9.0V: It draws ~3A, outputting around 30 Watts. (This is the most suitable voltage for optimal safe brightness).
  • At 12.0V: It draws ~4.2A, outputting around 48 Watts.

WARNING: If using a 12V supply to run it, you MUST use at least a 1.5 ohm 10W resistor in series to prevent burnout. However, using a DC-DC buck converter to step the 12V down to 9V is highly suggested.

Because of the high power rating, this COB panel generates substantial heat and must be mounted to an appropriate aluminum or copper heatsink with thermal paste for continuous use.

The 4S 30A 14.8V Lithium Battery BMS PCB Protection Board is a professional-grade battery management system designed to protect and optimize the performance of 4-series lithium-ion or lithium-polymer battery packs. With a continuous discharge capability of 30A, this board is specifically engineered for high-drain applications such as cordless electric drills, portable sprayers, and high-power LED lighting systems.

Equipped with 10 low-load resistance MOS tubes, the BMS ensures efficient power delivery while preventing overheating. One of its standout features is the integrated balance function, which ensures that each cell in the series is charged evenly, significantly extending the overall lifespan of your battery pack.

Safety is paramount with this module; it features an AUTO Recovery function and comprehensive protection against overcharging, over-discharging, and short circuits. Whether you are building a custom battery pack for power tools or replacing an existing BMS, the RBO-1138 provides a reliable and compact solution for your energy needs.

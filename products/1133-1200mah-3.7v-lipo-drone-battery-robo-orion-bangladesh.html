Frequently Asked Questions

This battery is widely compatible with RC Quadcopters that use a 3.7V power source with a JST plug, such as the Syma X5C, X5SC, X5SW, JJRC H5P, MJX, and Sky Viper models. Always check the battery dimensions to ensure it fits the battery bay.

It comes with a standard 2-Pin JST (red) connector, which is standard for most 3.7V RC toys and drones.

You must use a dedicated Li-Po charger (like a TP4056 module or a commercial RC charger). Never overcharge past 4.2V, and avoid discharging it below 3.0V to prevent permanent damage to the cells.

The battery measures approximately 52mm (length) x 30mm (width) x 10mm (thickness), weighing around 28-30 grams. Be sure to verify your drone's battery compartment size.

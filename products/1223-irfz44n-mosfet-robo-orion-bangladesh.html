The IRFZ44N is a highly popular, robust N-Channel Power MOSFET trusted by engineers and hobbyists alike for high-current switching applications. Known for its rugged design and extremely fast switching capabilities, this transistor is heavily used in DC motor drivers, high-power inverters, and switch-mode power supplies (SMPS).

Housed in an industry-standard TO-220AB package, the IRFZ44N can safely handle a continuous drain current (Id) of up to 49A and block drain-to-source voltages up to 55V. It boasts a very low static drain-to-source on-resistance (Rds(on)) of approximately 17.5mΩ, ensuring minimal power dissipation and high efficiency during operation.

Important Note for Microcontroller Users: Unlike its logic-level cousin (the IRLZ44N), the IRFZ44N has a standard gate threshold. While it may partially turn on with a 5V logic signal from an Arduino, it requires around 10V at the gate to fully saturate and achieve its lowest resistance. If you are driving heavy loads via a 5V microcontroller, utilizing a gate driver circuit or a secondary transistor is highly recommended to prevent the MOSFET from overheating.

The 4 Piece 8x20mm Coreless Motor with 55mm Propellers is the ultimate power kit for hobbyists venturing into the world of micro-drones. These 8020 magnetic coreless motors are engineered for extreme speed and lightweight performance, making them an ideal choice for quadcopters with frames around 100mm.

With a blistering rated speed of 39,000 RPM, these motors provide the necessary lift to get your micro-drone airborne and responsive. The coreless design ensures lower noise and minimal resistance compared to traditional brushed motors, although they are optimized for speed over torque.

This combo pack includes everything you need for a basic 4-motor setup, including precisely matched propellers to ensure stability and efficiency. Whether you are building a school project or a professional micro-drone prototype, these motors offer the reliability and performance required for stable flight.

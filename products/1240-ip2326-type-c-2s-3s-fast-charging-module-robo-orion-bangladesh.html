The IP2326 Type-C 2S/3S Lithium Battery Fast Charging Boost Module is an ultra-compact and highly efficient power management solution for makers and hobbyists. At its core is the advanced IP2326 synchronous switch boost charging IC, which intelligently takes a standard 5V USB Type-C input (or QC fast-charge source) and steps it up to efficiently charge either a 2S (8.4V) or 3S (12.6V) lithium-ion battery pack.

Capable of outputting up to 15W of fast-charging power, this module greatly reduces charging times compared to traditional linear chargers. One of its standout features is the integrated automatic cell balancing function when used in 2S mode, ensuring that both battery cells remain perfectly matched for a longer overall lifespan and safer operation.

The module includes onboard LED indicators to display charging status and is packed with fundamental battery protection features, including over-current, over-voltage, short circuit, and NTC temperature protection. Whether you're building a DIY power bank or upgrading the charging circuit in a robotic project, the IP2326 module provides safe, high-speed, and balanced energy conversion.

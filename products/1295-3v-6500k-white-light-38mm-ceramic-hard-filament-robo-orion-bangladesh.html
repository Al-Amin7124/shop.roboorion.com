The 3V 6500K Cool White Light 38mm Ceramic Hard LED Filament (commonly called an LED noodle or COB filament) is an advanced, rigid linear light source designed to deliver clean, modern daylight-white illumination to low-voltage custom designs. Measuring 38mm, this robust light strip is highly sought after by makers, cosplayers, architectural modelers, and design shops in Bangladesh for creating custom scale models, retro-style modern lamps, and low-current battery-operated crafts.

Operating on a nominal 3V DC supply (drawing between 100mA and 120mA), this filament offers exceptional energy efficiency while producing a crisp, vivid 6500K Cool White Light. Built using Chip-on-Glass (COG) technology on a rigid ceramic base, it dissipates heat incredibly well, allowing your projects to remain completely cool to the touch. This layout distributes light uniformly across a full 360-degree viewing angle, eliminating the harsh hotspots typical of standard SMD LEDs.

For painless wiring, the module features marked terminal soldering tabs on each end—the positive (+) end has a tiny pre-drilled hole, while the negative (-) terminal has a solid flat metal tab. Ideal for scale dollhouses, custom cosplay, sci-fi dioramas, and Edison bulb restoration, this durable ceramic filament merges vintage filament warmth with modern solid-state reliability.

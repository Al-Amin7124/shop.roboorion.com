The 9V Battery Connector (commonly referred to as a 9V battery snap, wire terminal, or battery clip harness) is a ubiquitous power supply breakout clip used extensively by students, electronics builders, and DIY hobbyists in Bangladesh. Ideal for breadboard prototyping, diagnostic tools, and portable microcontroller operations, this harness converts generic primary 9V block batteries into easily routed power leads.

Designed to accommodate standard 6F22, PP3, and 006P 9-Volt battery configurations, the adapter features double-stud press contacts plated with corrosion-resistant nickel-copper alloy. This ensures strong mechanical snap links that prevent intermittent connection issues or low-voltage line drops. The contacts are encased inside a protective, flexible soft vinyl/leather-textured top hood which insulates the terminals from accidental shorts and dust.

On the output end, the adapter comes pre-wired with an approximate 15cm (6 inches) dual-strand PVC insulated lead. It utilizes standard color conventions—Red for Positive (+9V) and Black for Negative (GND)—with tinned, pre-stripped ends (~5mm) allowing direct terminal block insertion, breadboard connection, or quick wire joint soldering.

Building custom high-power battery packs requires uncompromising safety. The 3S 30A Lithium Battery Balance BMS V1 from RC Product BD provides exceptional protection for 3-cell (11.1V/12.6V) lithium-ion and Li-Po battery packs. Unlike standard protection boards, this premium V1 model includes an integrated temperature sensor probe.

During heavy loads, batteries can heat up dangerously. This BMS actively monitors cell temperature and will automatically cut off the circuit if the heat reaches the 48-50°C threshold, preventing thermal runaway and potential fire hazards. Furthermore, it easily manages a continuous load of 30A and can withstand massive overload spikes up to 85A for 10 seconds (hard cut-off at 90A).

It also offers a precise short-circuit cut-off at 22A, alongside standard cell balancing to ensure each 18650 cell charges equally, maximizing your battery pack's lifespan. Backed by a 3-month warranty from RC Product BD, you can build with confidence.

The 12V 775 DC Motor is an incredibly versatile and powerful brushed motor renowned among hobbyists, engineers, and DIY makers. Operating flexibly on voltages ranging from 12V to 24V DC, this powerhouse can deliver blistering speeds of 10,000 RPM at 12V, accelerating up to 20,000 RPM when supplied with 24V.

Constructed for longevity and relentless heavy-duty performance, the motor features a high-quality front ball bearing that significantly reduces friction and noise during operation. Inside the sturdy metal casing, integrated cooling fan blades continuously channel airflow across the copper coils, protecting the motor against thermal breakdown during prolonged, high-torque tasks like drilling or cutting.

Pro Tip: Due to its massive torque, the 775 motor demands a high-discharge power supply. Standard 1A adapters will stall; always use a power supply or LiPo battery rated for at least 10A.

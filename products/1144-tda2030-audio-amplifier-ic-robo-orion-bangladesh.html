The TDA2030 Audio Amplifier IC is a monolithic integrated circuit designed for low-to-medium power audio applications. Whether you are building a compact desktop speaker system, a portable amplifier, or an active audio filter, the TDA2030 provides a stable and efficient solution for signal amplification.

Capable of delivering an output power between 14W and 18W, this IC is renowned for its low harmonic distortion, ensuring that audio output remains clean and natural. Its monolithic design simplifies the external circuitry required, making it highly accessible for DIY electronics hobbyists.

To protect the device from failure, the TDA2030 includes built-in thermal shutdown protection and short-circuit protection. This ensures that even under heavy load or accidental wiring faults, the IC remains safe, provided it is paired with an adequate aluminum heatsink.

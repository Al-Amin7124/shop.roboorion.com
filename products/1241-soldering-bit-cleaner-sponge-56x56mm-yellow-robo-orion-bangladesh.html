Usage Instructions

  • Do not use the sponge while it is dry.
  • Add water to the sponge until it expands fully.
  • Squeeze out the excess water so the sponge is damp, not soaking wet.
  • Wipe the hot soldering iron tip gently across the surface.
  • Re-tin the tip immediately after cleaning to prevent rapid oxidation.

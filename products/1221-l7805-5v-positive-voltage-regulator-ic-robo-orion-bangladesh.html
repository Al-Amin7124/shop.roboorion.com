If you are searching for the best "L7805 price in BD", you have found the definitive power regulation component. The L7805 (also labeled LM7805) is arguably the most famous and widely used three-terminal positive voltage regulator in the world. Its purpose is simple but critical: it takes an unregulated input voltage (between 7V and 35V) and steps it down to a rock-solid +5V DC output.

This 5V output is the lifeblood of digital logic circuits, making the L7805 an absolute necessity for powering Arduino boards, microcontrollers, 5V sensors, and TTL logic gates from higher voltage sources like 12V batteries or power adapters. Encased in a standard TO-220 package, the L7805 is highly robust and can deliver an output current of up to 1.5A when attached to an adequate heatsink.

Furthermore, it features built-in thermal overload protection and short-circuit current limiting. This means the IC will automatically throttle down or shut off to protect itself if it overheats or if a short circuit occurs in your project. It is the ultimate plug-and-play solution for DIY power supplies.

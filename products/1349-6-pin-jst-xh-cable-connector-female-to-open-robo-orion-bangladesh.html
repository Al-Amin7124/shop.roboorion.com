This pre-crimped wire harness features a standard JST XH female plug on one end, and open, bare wires on the other end, making your custom wiring jobs incredibly fast and secure.

With a universal 2.54mm (0.1 inch) pitch, the female connector perfectly slides onto standard male pin headers found on Arduino boards, sensor modules, and custom PCBs. The 6-pin configuration is extremely popular—it is the exact industry standard used for 5S Lithium Polymer (LiPo) battery balance charging leads. Measuring 20cm (approx. 8 inches) in length, the cable provides plenty of reach for your enclosures. The open ends are typically pre-stripped and lightly tinned, meaning they are ready to be soldered directly to your board or spliced to other wires immediately out of the box.

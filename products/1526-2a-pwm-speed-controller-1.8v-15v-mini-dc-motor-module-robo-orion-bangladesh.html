If you need precise control over your low-voltage DIY electronics projects, the 2A PWM Speed Controller (1.8V-15V) – Mini DC Motor Module is an absolute essential. Instead of using inefficient resistors that waste precious battery energy as heat, this ultra-compact module uses Pulse Width Modulation (PWM). It rapidly switches the power on and off to regulate the output, providing smooth, highly efficient speed control with practically zero power loss.

This controller boasts an incredibly versatile input range, safely operating from a remarkably low 1.8V up to 15V DC. With a 2A continuous current capacity, it can deliver up to 30 Watts of total power. This makes it the perfect solution for regulating the speed of small 3V toy motors, 5V cooling fans, and 12V DC water pumps. It is equally effective when used as a flicker-free dimmer switch for low-voltage LED light arrays.

Safety and ease-of-use are built right in. The module includes a 2A self-recovering (resettable) fuse to protect your electronics from accidental short circuits. The interface features a smooth rotary potentiometer knob that allows seamless duty cycle adjustment from 0% to 100%, and cleverly integrates a physical click-switch so you can completely cut power to the load without disconnecting your battery.

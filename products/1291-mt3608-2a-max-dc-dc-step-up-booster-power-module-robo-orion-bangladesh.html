The MT3608 2A Max DC-DC Step Up Booster Power Module is a highly-efficient, ultra-compact switching regulator designed to boost low DC input voltages into stable higher DC outputs. Ideal for powering electronic modules, digital displays, and microcontrollers from standard lithium batteries or USB ports, this module is highly sought after by makers, hobbyists, and electronic developers in Bangladesh.

Based on the high-performance MT3608 step-up controller, it supports input voltages from 2V to 24V DC. With a simple turn of the onboard multi-turn precision potentiometer, you can step up the output voltage up to 28V DC (the output voltage must always be greater than the input). This allows you to easily run 5V, 9V, 12V, or even 24V electronic units from a single 3.7V 18650 lithium battery.

The circuit operates at a high fixed switching frequency of 1.2MHz, allowing for a compact board size with minimal output ripple. To protect your circuitry, the board features internal soft-start logic, over-current thresholds, and thermal overload protection. Its tiny footprint makes it perfect for miniature project cases, custom solar trackers, and portable emergency power banks.

⚠️ CRITICAL CALIBRATION NOTICE FOR BEGINNERS:

When using this booster board for the first time, the potentiometer is configured at its maximum internal resistance. Rotating the screw will not show output changes initially. You must rotate the trimmer screw counterclockwise for 15 to 20 full turns before the step-up logic begins to trigger and raise output voltages.

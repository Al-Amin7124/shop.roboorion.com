The IRLZ44N logic level IRLZ44N MOSFET is an N-Channel Power MOSFET specifically designed for direct drive from microcontrollers. If you are working with an Arduino, Raspberry Pi, or ESP32, standard MOSFETs often require a dedicated 10V gate driver to fully turn on. The IRLZ44N eliminates this need, featuring a low gate threshold that saturates perfectly on a simple 5V logic signal.

Despite its low drive voltage requirements, it packs serious power. This TO-220 packaged transistor can handle a continuous drain current (Id) of up to 47A and block voltages up to 55V. It boasts a very low static drain-to-source on-resistance (Rds(on)) of just 22mΩ, meaning it generates minimal heat and operates highly efficiently during rapid switching tasks.

Whether you are building a custom motor driver, managing high-power LED strips via PWM dimming, or switching heavy DC loads, the IRLZ44N is one of the most reliable and easy-to-use power transistors for modern hobbyist and engineering projects.

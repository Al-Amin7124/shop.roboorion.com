For DIY builders looking for an affordable 4S 30A LiFePO4 BMS price in Bangladesh, this comprehensive 4-string Battery Management System delivers heavy-duty protection for 3.2V Lithium Iron Phosphate cells. Designed strictly for a 4S (12.8V nominal) battery configuration, this PCB efficiently manages high power demands, offering a robust 30A continuous discharge rate and supporting massive instantaneous power spikes up to 56A.

Battery longevity and safety are ensured through the BMS’s rigorous monitoring. The board intelligently stops charging when cells reach 3.75V (±0.05V) and cuts off discharge at 2.1V (±0.08V) to prevent permanent deep-discharge damage. Additionally, it features a built-in balance circuit that activates at 3.60V, distributing a steady 58mA balance current across cells to keep the entire battery bank in perfect synchronization.

Compactly sized at 56x47mm, this protection board fits comfortably inside most custom battery enclosures. It comes complete with a balance cable, making it an excellent all-in-one solution for upgrading power wheels, building custom solar storage banks, and creating dependable 12V LiFePO4 replacements for heavy lead-acid batteries.

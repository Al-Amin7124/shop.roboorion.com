Never let a power cut interrupt your internet connection again! The Type C Mini Portable Router UPS is the ultimate budget-friendly power backup solution in Bangladesh. Designed to provide uninterrupted power to your networking devices during load shedding, this compact unit automatically switches to battery power the moment the electricity goes out.

At the core of this device is the highly reliable J5019 Boost/Charge UPS Module paired with a robust 3500mAh lithium-ion battery. It solves the most common problem for internet users by featuring two dedicated 12V outputs, allowing you to power both your WiFi Router and your Optical Network Unit (ONU/Media Converter) simultaneously. Depending on your devices' power draw, you can expect between 1 to 4 hours of solid backup time.

Charging is incredibly simple thanks to the modern 5V Type-C input port. You can plug it into a standard smartphone wall adapter. It also features a convenient integrated ON/OFF switch, giving you complete control over the power supply to your devices.

The TP4056 1A Li-Ion Battery Charging Module with Type-C input and current protection is a compact, reliable module designed for charging single-cell 3.7V lithium-ion batteries (1Ah or higher), such as 18650 cells. Based on the TP4056 charger IC and DW01 battery protection IC, this module delivers 1A charge current and automatically cuts off when charging is complete.

The integrated protection IC safeguards the battery in multiple ways: when battery voltage drops below 2.4V, the load is disconnected to prevent harmful over-discharge. The module also protects against over-voltage and reverse polarity connection — it is designed to absorb fault conditions rather than damage the battery.

Every module is fully tested before shipment, ensuring high reliability and consistent performance. The TP4056 IC also features a current monitor, under-voltage lockout, automatic recharge, and two status pins to indicate charge termination and input voltage presence.

How to Use the TP4056 Module

  1. Connect a Type-C cable for power, or apply 5V DC to the pads marked IN+ and IN- on the left-hand side of the module.
  2. Connect the cell to charge to the B+/B- pads on the right-hand side of the module.
  3. A load (something for the battery to power) can be connected to the OUT+/OUT- pads on the right-hand side.
  4. Important: Disconnect the load when charging.
  5. The red LED indicates charging in progress; the green LED indicates charging has finished.
  6. Never charge your battery at a rate greater than 1C.

Important Notes

  1. An ammeter can only be connected to the 5V input end of the module.
  2. Optimal charging current is approximately 37% of battery capacity. For a 1000mAh battery, 400mA is sufficient.
  3. The connecting wire should not be too thick.
  4. Ensure all connection points are secure.
  5. If input voltage exceeds 5.2V, the current will drop below 1000mA — this is normal auto-protection behavior to prevent chip damage.
  6. Do NOT connect in reverse polarity.

Features

  1. LED indicator: Red = charging, Green = fully charged.
  2. Over-current protection: Yes.
  3. Reverse polarity: NOT supported — do not connect in reverse.
  4. Uses the TP4056 charging IC with minimal peripheral circuitry for reliable, high-accuracy charging.
  5. Fully automated surface-mount manufacturing process for consistency.

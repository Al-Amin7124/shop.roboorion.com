The VISERO Original 12V 5A Smart Fast Lead Acid Battery Charger (1205) with Auto Cut-Off is a high-performance automatic charger engineered to provide safe, fast, and efficient power replenishment for standard 12V batteries in Bangladesh. Utilizing an advanced microprocessor controller and three-phase charging technology, this unit represents an indispensable tool for maintaining the life expectancy of car, motorcycle, bike, IPS, solar systems, table fans, and LED backup cells.

Equipped with automatic Pulse Width Modulation (PWM) stop-charge logic, the VISERO 1205 actively monitors battery voltage and drops into a safe float phase (auto cut-off) once the battery is fully replenished. This locks the battery out from dangerous overcharging cycles and prevents slow acid degradation or drying. Additionally, on-board LED indicators show real-time charging statuses, directly reflecting the cell's current charging phase for seamless user tracking.

Built-in diagnostic safety protocols run continuously under the hood, protecting the unit against short-circuits, overcharge, over-voltage, and reverse polarity wiring faults. Compatible with standard dry, wet, gel, and maintenance-free lead-acid battery capacities spanning from 7Ah up to 100Ah, this premium charger is built to withstand domestic voltage fluctuations and deliver top-tier reliability.

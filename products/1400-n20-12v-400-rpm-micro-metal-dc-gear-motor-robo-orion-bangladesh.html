The N20 12V 400 RPM Micro Metal DC Gear Motor is a highly popular, compact, and extremely durable power source for miniature electronics projects. Unlike standard plastic gear motors, this N20 variant features an all-metal gearbox. This ensures a fantastic power-to-weight ratio and provides significantly higher torque, allowing it to drive heavier loads without stripping the internal gears.

Operating at an optimal 12V DC, this specific model delivers a fast no-load speed of 400 RPM. This makes it an ideal choice for fast-moving DIY robots like line followers and maze solvers, where speed is prioritized over sheer lifting force. It features a standard 3mm D-shaped shaft, making it incredibly easy to fit matching N20 wheels, pulleys, and structural couplers tightly without slippage.

Whether you are building a custom smart car, an automated electronic door lock, or a motorized 3D printed mechanism, the N20 motor offers the reliability, precision, and longevity required by advanced makers and engineering students.

The Uni-T UT33A+ is a premium, entry-level digital multimeter that redefines the standard for pocket-sized diagnostic tools in Bangladesh. Designed for both professional technicians and electronics hobbyists, it steps away from frustrating manual dials by offering full auto-ranging capabilities. Simply select your measurement type, and the meter automatically adjusts to the correct range.

Safety and durability are at the core of the UT33+ series. Housed in an ergonomic protective cover with a built-in test lead hanging groove, it is certified to withstand a 2-meter drop test and operate safely in CAT II 600V environments. It easily handles AC/DC voltages up to 600V and currents up to 10A.

Unlike many basic multimeters, the UT33A+ features a dedicated 2mF capacitance testing mode, crucial for modern PCB repair and diagnostics. It also includes an auto-backlight display, loud continuity buzzer, diode check, and runs on two easily replaceable 1.5V AAA batteries, ensuring you always have reliable power.

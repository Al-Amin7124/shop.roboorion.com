The DC-5V-35V 5A PWM Motor Speed Controller is a highly efficient mini DC motor speed regulator designed to provide precise control over DC motors by adjusting Pulse-Width Modulation (PWM). Capable of handling a wide operating voltage range from 4.5V to 35V and a continuous output current of up to 5A, this compact module can deliver up to 90W of power.

It features a built-in potentiometer with a knob, allowing for smooth speed regulation across a 1% to 100% duty cycle. The high 20kHz PWM frequency ensures that motor operation remains quiet and efficient without unpleasant humming noises. It is an ideal component for robotics, DIY electronics, and automated hobbyist projects requiring variable speed logic.

Precautions: The input must be DC. Do not connect this module directly to AC power (e.g., 220V AC mains), or it will burn instantly. Ensure proper polarity (+/-) during connection to prevent damage to the governor. If the motor rotation direction needs to be reversed, simply swap the motor's connection wires.

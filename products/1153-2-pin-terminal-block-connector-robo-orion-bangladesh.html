The 2 Pin PCB Terminal Block Connector is a high-reliability electrical connector designed for secure and easy wire-to-PCB connections. Featuring a standard 5.08mm pitch, this connector is an essential component for hobbyists, engineers, and makers who require a stable and vibration-resistant connection for power inputs or signal wiring.

Constructed from heat-resistant PBT (Polybutylene terephthalate), the block ensures excellent insulation and durability even in challenging thermal environments. The pin headers are made of tin-plated brass, and the internal contacts are nickel-plated brass, significantly reducing corrosion and enhancing conductivity over time.

Each pin is equipped with a precise screw mechanism that allows users to fasten wires firmly without the need for soldering the wires themselves, making maintenance and replacements quick and efficient. This connector is widely used in robotics, industrial control panels, and DIY electronic power modules.

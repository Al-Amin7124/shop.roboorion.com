The Robo Orion 4.2V 3A AC 220V 1S Lithium Battery Charger Module is a compact, high-efficiency AC-to-DC charging board engineered to fast-charge single-cell (1S) 3.7V / 4.2V lithium-ion and lithium-polymer batteries directly from standard 220V AC household electricity. It is designed specifically as a self-contained charger power supply, eliminating the need for bulky external DC adapters.

Equipped with an intelligent two-stage CC/CV (Constant Current / Constant Voltage) controller, this board delivers a rapid 3A charging current until the battery reaches its 4.2V float threshold. Once fully charged, the smart auto cut-off circuit terminates the charge cycle, preventing overcharging, heat buildup, and battery degradation. An integrated dual-color LED provides clear visual status: illuminating Red during active charging and switching to Green once the cell is completely full or idling in standby.

Important Notice: This module is an AC-DC charging power supply circuit with auto-cutoff; it is not a BMS protection board. It does not provide load-side over-discharge or short-circuit cutoff while the battery powers appliances. It is ideally suited for building or repairing high-power rechargeable LED emergency lights, solar torches, portable fan battery bays, Bluetooth speakers, and custom 1S 18650 / 21700 battery charging stations in Bangladesh.

Frequently Asked Questions

This module is an AC-DC charging power supply board, NOT a BMS (Battery Management System). It connects directly to 220V AC mains to charge 1S 3.7V/4.2V lithium-ion batteries with automatic full-charge cut-off at 4.2V. It does not provide low-voltage protection during load discharge.

The module operates on a precise CC/CV (Constant Current / Constant Voltage) curve. It delivers up to 3A constant current until the battery reaches 4.2V, then gradually tapers the charging current down and cuts off power completely once fully charged.

The integrated LED illuminates Red during active charging. Once the battery reaches 4.2V and the auto cut-off circuit engages, or when the board is idling without a load, the indicator switches to Green.

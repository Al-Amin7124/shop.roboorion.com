Unleash your creativity with the 3V 130mm flexible LED filament 2700K price in Bangladesh that fits any maker's budget. Often referred to as "LED noodles", these flexible light sources are the perfect component for building custom vintage neon-style signs, crafting unique Edison-style lamps, and creating cozy ambiance art lighting.

Unlike standard hard ceramic filaments, this 130mm filament is constructed on an FPC (Flexible Printed Circuit) substrate and encapsulated in a durable, light-diffusing phosphor silicone shell. This design allows you to bend, curve, and loop the light into intricate shapes without breaking the internal circuit. Emitting a beautifully warm 2700K Warm White glow, it perfectly mimics the aesthetic of classic antique tungsten wire while providing 360-degree omnidirectional illumination.

Operating on a safe 3V DC power supply, it is incredibly easy to integrate into portable, battery-powered electronic projects. Simply solder wires to the positive (anode) and negative (cathode) ends (distinguished by the hole punched in the anode tab), add a suitable current-limiting resistor, and watch your DIY vintage art come to life.

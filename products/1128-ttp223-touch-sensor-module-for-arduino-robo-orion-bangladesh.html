The TTP223 Touch Sensor Module is an ultra-compact capacitive touch switch based on the popular TTP223 touch-detecting IC. It acts as an excellent electronic replacement for traditional mechanical push-buttons, providing a modern interface for your DIY and Arduino projects.

The sensor is highly versatile. By utilizing the 'A' and 'B' solder pads on the PCB, you can easily configure the sensor to operate in either momentary or latching (toggle) modes, and switch the default output between active-high and active-low logic. It consumes very low power and provides a quick response time of about 60ms in fast mode.

Due to its capacitive nature, this module can detect touches through thin non-metallic materials like glass, plastic, or acrylic sheets, allowing you to create sleek, hidden buttons for smart home panels, custom lamps, and interactive models.

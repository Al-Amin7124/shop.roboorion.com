The L7812 (often referred to interchangeably as LM7812) is an incredibly popular three-terminal positive voltage regulator IC. It is designed to take an unregulated, higher input voltage (typically between 14V and 35V) and output a clean, stable +12V DC. This makes it an absolute necessity for powering 12V logic circuits, cooling fans, motor drivers, and audio amplifiers from a higher voltage battery or power supply.

Encased in a standard TO-220 package, the L7812 is highly robust. It can deliver an output current of up to 1.5A when attached to an adequate heatsink. Furthermore, it features built-in thermal overload protection and short-circuit current limiting, meaning the IC will automatically shut down rather than destroy itself if it gets too hot or if the output is accidentally shorted.

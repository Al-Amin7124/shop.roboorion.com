If you are moving past breadboard modules and designing your own custom PCBs, finding the best AMS1117 3.3V IC price in Bangladesh is crucial. The AMS1117-3.3 is the undisputed industry standard for Low Dropout (LDO) linear voltage regulators. Packaged in a compact SMD SOT-223 format, this bare component effortlessly drops a 5V input down to a perfectly stable 3.3V output.

This chip is the very heart of almost all 3.3V sensor modules, NodeMCU ESP8266 boards, and ESP32 dev boards. While the manufacturer rates it for a maximum of 1A, practical continuous use should be kept between 500mA and 800mA, provided your PCB has an adequately sized copper pad connected to the Tab (Pin 2) for thermal dissipation.

Whether you are replacing a blown regulator on a faulty Arduino clone, or manufacturing a batch of custom IoT sensor nodes, stocking up on these highly affordable, reliable SMD regulators is an absolute necessity.

Note: This is the bare SMD component, not a plug-and-play module. You must solder it to a PCB and use appropriate bypass capacitors (e.g., 10µF) on the input and output to prevent voltage oscillation.

Features

  1. Fixed 3.3V output, no external voltage setting resistors required.
  2. Low Dropout Voltage: Only 1.1V drop at maximum load.
  3. Standard SOT-223 Surface Mount (SMD) package for easy automated or hand soldering.
  4. Built-in internal thermal shutdown and current limiting protections.

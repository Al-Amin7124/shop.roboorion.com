Frequently Asked Questions

This comprehensive assortment includes tubes with internal diameters ranging from 1mm up to 10mm. This variety ensures you have the right size for tiny electronic component leads as well as thicker power cables.

These high-quality polyolefin heat shrink tubes feature a 2:1 shrinkage ratio. This means they will shrink down to exactly half of their original diameter when heat is applied, providing a tight, secure seal around your wires.

While a hot air heat gun is recommended for the most even and professional finish, you can also use a standard lighter, a soldering iron's radiant heat, or a high-power hair dryer in a pinch to activate the shrinkage.

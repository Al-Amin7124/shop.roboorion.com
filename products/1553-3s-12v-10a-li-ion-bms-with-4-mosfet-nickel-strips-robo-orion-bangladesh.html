Frequently Asked Questions

The 4-MOSFET configuration distributes current evenly across low-resistance switching stages, significantly reducing internal impedance (<60 mΩ) and thermal buildup while sustaining a true 10A continuous discharge.

The integrated nickel strips eliminate the need for delicate wire soldering onto tiny PCB pads. You can spot-weld or solder the nickel tabs directly to your 18650/21700 cell terminals, reducing assembly time and preventing heat damage.

Charge the pack using a 12.6V DC CC/CV lithium-ion charger. Standard 12V lead-acid chargers should not be used as their voltage float and charge curves differ from 3S lithium requirements.

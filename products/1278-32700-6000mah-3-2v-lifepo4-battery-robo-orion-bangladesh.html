Looking for a safe, ultra-long-lasting energy solution? The 32700 6000mAh 3.2V LiFePO4 battery is the industry standard for high-capacity energy storage. Unlike standard 3.7V Lithium-ion cells (like the 18650), Lithium Iron Phosphate (LiFePO4) chemistry offers unmatched thermal stability, meaning it will not catch fire or explode even under harsh conditions. It is the premier choice for custom battery banks in Bangladesh.

Boasting a massive 6000mAh (6.0Ah) capacity within its cylindrical 32mm x 70mm frame, this cell is designed for heavy-duty applications. What truly sets LiFePO4 apart is its cycle life. You can expect over 2000 charge and discharge cycles with minimal capacity loss, making it significantly more cost-effective over time compared to traditional lead-acid or standard lithium batteries.

Whether you are building a solar energy storage system, a robust backup power bank for networking gear, or a high-discharge custom EV pack for electric scooters, the 32700 cell delivers steady 3.2V power. Be sure to pair these cells with a compatible 3.2V LiFePO4 BMS to ensure proper voltage balancing and protection.

The 8.2V 1/4W Zener Diode is a fundamental discrete semiconductor component used widely in electronic circuit design. Enclosed in a standard DO-35 axial glass package, this diode is specifically engineered to operate in the reverse breakdown region, making it an excellent choice for creating reliable voltage references and simple voltage regulators.

With a power dissipation rating of 0.25 Watts (250mW), this Zener diode effectively clamps voltage spikes, protecting sensitive microcontrollers, operational amplifiers, and logic ICs from transient over-voltage damage. It is a vital component for clipping circuits, waveform shaping, and power supply stabilization in both educational lab setups and commercial prototypes.

The through-hole (axial lead) design ensures it is perfectly breadboard-friendly for quick testing, while its robust glass casing ensures consistent performance over a wide temperature range.

Frequently Asked Questions

A Zener diode is primarily used as a voltage regulator or a voltage reference. When operated in reverse bias (beyond its Zener breakdown voltage of 8.2V), it maintains a constant 8.2V across its terminals, making it perfect for protecting sensitive circuits from over-voltage.

It depends on your circuit's power dissipation. If your circuit dissipates less than 0.25W (250mW) across the diode, you can use a 1/4W Zener. However, if the circuit requires the diode to dissipate more than 250mW, a 1/4W diode will overheat and fail. Always check your load calculations.

This diode comes in a standard DO-35 axial glass package. It has through-hole leads, making it very easy to bend and insert into breadboards, perfboards, or custom printed circuit boards (PCBs).

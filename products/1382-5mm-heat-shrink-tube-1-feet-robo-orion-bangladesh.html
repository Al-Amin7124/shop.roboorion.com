Our 5mm Heat Shrink Tube provides a fast, safe, and professional way to insulate your electrical joints, repair damaged wires, and protect small electronic components. This tube comes in a convenient 1-foot length and acts as an essential item for any electronics hobbyist or professional.

Made from high-quality polyolefin, this heat shrink tube features a standard 2:1 shrink ratio. Once heated, it will cleanly shrink down to approximately half its original diameter (from 5mm down to 2.5mm), creating a secure, weather-resistant, and electrically insulated barrier over the targeted wire or connection.

The 5mm size is exceptionally versatile and well-suited for insulating standard gauge wires used in robotics, Arduino projects, internal power cables, and standard DC connections.

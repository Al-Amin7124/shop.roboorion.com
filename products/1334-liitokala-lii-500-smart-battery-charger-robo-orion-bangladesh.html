If you are building custom battery packs, power banks, or just need a reliable way to charge and verify your cells, the LiitoKala Lii-500 Smart Battery Charger is the industry standard. This powerful device is not just a charger—it is a highly accurate capacity tester. With four completely independent channels, you can charge, fast test, or 'Normal Test' four different batteries of different chemistries simultaneously.

The standout feature is the Normal Test mode. Instead of guessing a battery's health, this mode fully charges the cell, completely discharges it while measuring the exact energy extracted, and then recharges it to full. The built-in LCD screen will display real-time voltage, current, internal resistance, and the exact true capacity (mAh) of your 18650, 26650, 21700, or AA/AAA batteries.

Additionally, the Lii-500 features a 5V USB output port, allowing it to act as a power bank to charge your phone when populated with charged Li-ion cells.

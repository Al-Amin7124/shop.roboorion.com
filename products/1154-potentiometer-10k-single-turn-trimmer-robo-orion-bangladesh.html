The 10k Ohm Single-Turn Trimmer Potentiometer (code 103) is a precision variable resistor designed for fine-tuning electronic circuits. Unlike standard potentiometers, this trimmer is specifically built for "set-and-forget" calibrations, where a screwdriver is used to adjust the resistance to a specific value during the assembly or tuning phase of a project.

Featuring a cermet construction, this component offers excellent linearity and stability. Its single-turn design allows for a full range of resistance adjustment from near 0 to 10,000 ohms within one complete rotation, making it highly efficient for tight spaces and quick setups. The breadboard-friendly long leads ensure that it can be securely integrated into prototyping boards without the need for immediate soldering.

Housed in a compact, heat-resistant plastic package, it is durable enough for a variety of environments. Whether you are calibrating a sensor, adjusting the gain of an amplifier, or setting a timing threshold in a microcontroller project, this 103 trimmer provides the reliability and precision required for professional-grade DIY electronics.

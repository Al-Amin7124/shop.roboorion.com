The 3V Red LED Filament 38mm is a premium quality, energy-efficient ceramic hard filament designed for vintage style Edison bulbs, custom decorative lamps, retro indicator lights, and various creative DIY electronics projects in Bangladesh. This rigid 38mm filament consists of high-efficiency micro-LED chips encapsulated on a solid, heat-resistant ceramic substrate, guaranteeing high reliability and stability in creative illumination setups.

Developed with advanced Chip-on-Board (COB) technology, this LED filament distributes a rich and uniform red light over its entire length, creating an exquisite retro aesthetic reminiscent of traditional hot-glowing tungsten wire filaments. With its minimal forward voltage of just 3V, you can run this filament using simple battery layouts (such as two AA batteries in series) or standard microcontroller outputs, completely removing the safety challenges and power complexities of high-voltage lighting components.

Whether you are building custom indicator signs, repairing a vintage glass lamp, adding illuminated accents to scale models, or assembling school engineering projects, the 3V Red Ceramic Hard LED Filament offers excellent light spread, lower current draw, and superior heat management compared to conventional tungsten wires. It serves as a great bridge between classic vintage design and contemporary energy-saving technology.

The XH-M602 (V1874) Digital Battery Charge Control Module is a robust and highly adaptable solution for managing the charging cycles of virtually any battery. This module actively monitors real-time battery voltage and utilizes an onboard relay to switch the 220V AC power supply to your charger on or off. By setting custom upper and lower voltage limits, it effectively protects your batteries from damaging overcharge and extends their overall lifespan.

Equipped with a bright, 3-digit seven-segment LED display, the XH-M602 provides an impressive reading and control accuracy of 0.1V. It is universally compatible with lead-acid, lithium-ion, Li-Po, and LiFePO4 batteries operating anywhere within the DC 3.7V to 120V range. The intuitive push-button interface makes it incredibly easy to program the specific 'Start' and 'Stop' charging thresholds required by your unique battery bank.

Designed for durability and safety, the module features a high-quality plastic transformer offering corrosion and moisture resistance, along with heavy-duty screw terminals for secure electrical connections. It is an indispensable tool for custom solar charging setups, UPS systems, and DIY electric vehicle maintenance.

Frequently Asked Questions

The XH-M602 V1874 Digital Battery Charge Control Module is available for BDT 350 at Robo Orion BD.

The XH-M602 module acts as an intelligent relay switch. It continuously monitors the connected battery's voltage. When the battery reaches the preset 'Stop Voltage', the onboard relay disconnects the 220V AC input to the charger, effectively stopping the charge to prevent overcharging. When the voltage drops below the 'Start Voltage', it turns the charger back on.

The module is incredibly versatile and can be used to control the charging of any battery type (Lead-acid, Lithium-ion, Li-Po, LiFePO4) operating within the DC 3.7V to 120V range.

To set the Start Voltage, long-press the 'Start' button until the LED display flashes, then use the buttons to adjust the value. To set the Stop Voltage, long-press the 'Stop' button until it flashes, and adjust. Pressing both buttons simultaneously will reset the module to factory defaults.

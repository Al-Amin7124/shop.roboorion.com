If you are looking to build a high-fidelity DIY audio project, the primary search for a 3 inch 30W full range speaker price in Bangladesh leads you to this premium driver by HB. This 3-inch (approx. 81mm outer diameter) speaker is specifically engineered for full-range audio projection with a strong emphasis on punchy, warm mid-bass.

Constructed with a high-quality combination paper diaphragm (basin) and a massive 72x15mm external magnetic steel circuit, this speaker delivers crystal clear vocals and surprisingly deep lows for its size. The 4-Ohm impedance allows it to extract maximum power from portable Class D amplifiers (like the TPA3116D2 or XY-P15W modules), ensuring high volume without distortion.

Whether you are constructing a custom Bluetooth speaker, upgrading your car audio system, or building a home theater soundbar from scratch, this round, four-corner mount driver provides professional-grade acoustics in a compact form factor.

Note: To achieve the best bass response, this speaker must be mounted in a properly sealed or ported wooden/MDF enclosure. Operating it in "free air" will result in reduced bass output.

The DT-830D Digital Multimeter is an indispensable testing tool for engineering students, electronics hobbyists, and home DIYers in Bangladesh. This affordable yet highly reliable meter provides accurate readings for DC and AC voltage, DC current, and electrical resistance, making it perfect for basic circuit troubleshooting and educational labs.

One of the most valuable features of the 'D' variant (compared to the basic 830B) is the built-in continuity buzzer. This feature allows you to test for broken wires, faulty traces, and blown fuses with an instant audible alert, eliminating the need to constantly look at the screen while probing. The device also includes diode and hFE transistor testing functions.

Weighing around 170g and featuring a compact, pocket-friendly design, the DT-830D is easy to carry anywhere. The package includes a set of red and black test leads and is powered by a standard 9V battery, providing a clear 3 ½ digit reading on its LCD screen with built-in overload protection.

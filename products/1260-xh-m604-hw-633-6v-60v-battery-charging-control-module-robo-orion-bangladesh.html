If you need a reliable and customizable way to manage battery charging, the XH-M604 (also known as HW-633) is an essential component. This highly versatile 6V-60V Battery Charging Control Module acts as an automated protection switch between your power source (charger/solar panel) and your battery bank. It is widely used to prevent overcharging by automatically cutting off the connection when the battery reaches a safe upper voltage limit.

Using the onboard tactile buttons and bright digital display, you can freely set both the 'start charging' voltage and the 'stop charging' voltage with an impressive precision of ±0.1V. For example, on a 12V battery system, you can program the module to trigger the heavy-duty 30A relay to start charging when the voltage drops to 12.0V, and safely disconnect the power when it peaks at 14.4V. This smart automation ensures you save electricity while drastically extending the operational lifespan of your battery packs.

The XH-M604 is universally compatible with almost any chemistry, making it ideal for Lithium-ion, LiFePO4, Gel, and Lead-Acid storage batteries operating within the 6V to 60V threshold. It is highly recommended for household inverter charging setups, DIY solar charge systems, wind turbine controllers, and custom electric vehicle power stations.

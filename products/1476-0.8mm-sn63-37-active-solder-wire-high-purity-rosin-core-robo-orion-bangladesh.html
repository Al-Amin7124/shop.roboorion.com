If you require precision and reliability for your electronics projects, the 0.8mm Sn63/37 Active Solder Wire – High Purity Rosin Core offers the perfect balance of performance and ease-of-use. At just 0.8mm in diameter, this fine-gauge wire provides exceptional control when feeding solder into tight PCB traces, small through-hole components, and delicate Surface Mount Devices (SMD) where standard 1.0mm wire would be too thick.

This high-purity solder uses a professional-grade Sn63/Pb37 eutectic alloy. Unlike other mixtures, a eutectic alloy melts completely and solidifies instantly at exactly 183°C. This instant transition eliminates the "plastic phase," drastically reducing the chances of creating weak, brittle, or cold solder joints on your circuit boards.

In addition, the wire boasts an advanced active rosin core that cleans oxidation from component legs and copper pads the moment it melts. Designed as a "no-clean" flux, any residual traces left behind are non-conductive and non-corrosive, meaning your finished boards will look clean and professional without requiring a chemical wash.

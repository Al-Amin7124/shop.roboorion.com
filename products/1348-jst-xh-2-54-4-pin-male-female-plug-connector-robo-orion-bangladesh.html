If you are working on 3D printers, custom lithium battery packs, or Arduino electronics, finding the correct JST XH 2.54mm 4-pin connector price in Bangladesh is crucial for a clean build. The JST XH series is the absolute industry standard for low-voltage power transmission and signal routing.

This set includes both the female plug and the male PCB header. The 2.54mm (0.1 inch) pitch is a universal standard, meaning the male header can be soldered directly onto perfboards or plugged into standard breadboards for rapid prototyping. The female plug clicks securely into the male header, providing a friction-locked connection that will not vibrate loose in moving projects like drones or 3D printers.

With 4 pins, this connector is the exact size required for 3S (3-cell) LiPo battery balance leads, NEMA 17 stepper motor cables, and 4-wire I2C/SPI sensor modules (like OLED displays or Gyroscopes).

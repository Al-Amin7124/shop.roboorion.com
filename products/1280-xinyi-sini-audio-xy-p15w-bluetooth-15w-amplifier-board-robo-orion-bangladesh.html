If you are looking for an affordable XY-P15W Bluetooth amplifier board price in Bangladesh, this compact module by Xinyi Sini Audio is exactly what your DIY audio project needs. The XY-P15W is a Class D digital stereo amplifier that provides pristine, high-fidelity sound. Featuring the advanced Bluetooth 5.0 protocol, it ensures rapid connection, ultra-low latency, and a solid wireless range of up to 15 meters without signal drops.

Despite its small size (just 50x35mm), this amplifier packs a serious punch. Operating on a flexible DC 8V to 24V power supply, it can output a crisp 15W + 15W stereo sound (using an 8-ohm speaker at 16V). If you push the voltage to 24V with a 4-ohm speaker and add an external heatsink, it can achieve a peak output of up to 30W + 30W. It is highly versatile and capable of driving a wide range of bookshelf, car, or custom enclosure speakers.

The board is incredibly user-friendly. It features a built-in rotary wheel potentiometer for physical volume control—turning left or right adjusts the sound, while pressing down acts as a Play/Pause button. Additionally, the integrated circuitry is equipped with multiple self-recovery safety features, including reverse connection protection, short-circuit protection, and thermal shutdown, ensuring your amplifier and speakers are always safe.

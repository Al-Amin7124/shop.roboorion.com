The Mini-360 DC-DC Buck Converter is an ultra-compact, high-efficiency step-down voltage regulator built around the advanced MP2307DN IC. Despite its tiny footprint (measuring just 17x11mm), it is capable of dropping high inputs (up to 23V DC) down to a stable, adjustable output anywhere between 1.0V and 17V DC. This makes it an absolute essential for embedded electronics and DIY maker projects.

Thanks to its high switching frequency of 340kHz and Pulse-Width Modulation (PWM) technology, the Mini-360 achieves an impressive conversion efficiency of up to 95%. This drastically minimizes heat generation, allowing it to provide a solid 1.8A continuous current (and up to 3A surge) without the need for a bulky heatsink. Built-in 160°C thermal shutdown adds an extra layer of safety to your circuits.

Its incredibly small size and lightweight design (approx. 1g) make it the perfect choice for space-constrained applications. It easily steps down 12V batteries to power 5V Arduino boards, Raspberry Pi setups, communication modules, and drone flight controllers. Adjusting the output is as simple as turning the onboard micro-potentiometer.

If you are looking for the best 830 point breadboard price in BD, the MB102 full-size solderless breadboard is the ultimate prototyping tool for any electronics lab. Designed for rapidly building and testing circuits, it allows hobbyists, students, and engineers to easily plug in components like resistors, diodes, transistors, LEDs, and jumper wires without ever picking up a soldering iron.

This specific board features a total of 830 connection points arranged in the industry-standard 0.1-inch grid pattern. It is split into two main sections: a central terminal area containing 630 tie points for component placement, and two outer distribution strips providing 200 tie points specifically for continuous power and ground lines. Colored coordinates and lines make component mapping visually intuitive and organized.

Built with an ABS plastic body and internally equipped with phosphor bronze nickel-plated spring clips, it ensures a tight, conductive grip on standard 20-29 AWG wires. Completely reusable and highly cost-effective, the MB102 remains the standard starting block for testing Arduino circuits, sensor integrations, and custom logic designs.

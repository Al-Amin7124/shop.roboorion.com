The DC 0-100V Voltmeter LED Display Panel is an ultra-compact, high-precision digital voltage meter featuring a versatile 3-wire design. By separating the measuring signal wire from the power supply wires, this meter overcomes the limitations of 2-wire setups and is capable of accurately measuring voltages all the way down to a true 0V. The wide measuring range stretches from 0V up to an impressive 100V DC.

To power the display itself, the module simply needs a safe working voltage between 4V and 30V DC across the red and black wires. This flexibility allows you to power it from an independent source or tie it directly to your test subject. Additionally, the meter is built with reverse connection protection, ensuring that accidentally swapping the power wires will not burn or damage the module. The screen features a bright red 3-digit LED layout that provides exceptional legibility.

To optimize measurement accuracy, the voltmeter automatically shifts the position of the decimal point based on the measured voltage level, providing 0.01V resolution for low voltages. This module is an excellent choice for monitoring the battery statuses of motorcycles, automobiles, and DIY power supplies where precision across a large scale is demanded.

Wiring Instructions

  • Red Wire: Connect to the positive terminal of the display's power supply (4V-30V DC).
  • Black Wire: Connect to the common ground (negative).
  • Yellow (or White) Wire: Connect to the positive terminal of the voltage you wish to measure (0-100V DC).
  • 2-Wire Bypass: Tie Red and Yellow together to measure their shared power source (limits measuring range to 4V-30V).

If you need precise control over your electronics projects, the 5A PWM Speed Controller (3V-35V) – DC Motor Control Module is an essential tool for your workbench. Instead of using inefficient resistors that waste energy as heat, this module uses Pulse Width Modulation (PWM) technology. This means it turns the power on and off thousands of times a second to regulate output, providing smooth, high-efficiency control with almost zero power loss.

This controller boasts an incredibly wide input voltage range, safely operating anywhere from a low 3V up to a massive 35V DC. Combined with its 5A continuous current capacity, it can deliver up to 90 Watts of total power. This makes it an incredibly versatile switch, perfect for regulating the speed of small robotics motors, adjusting the flow of DC water pumps, or acting as a smooth dimmer switch for LED light strips.

The interface is designed for user-friendliness. It features a rotary potentiometer knob that allows you to seamlessly adjust the duty cycle from 1% to 100%. For added convenience, the knob integrates a physical click-switch function, allowing you to turn the connected device completely off without unplugging the power source. Compact and easy to wire, it is a favorite among makers in Bangladesh.

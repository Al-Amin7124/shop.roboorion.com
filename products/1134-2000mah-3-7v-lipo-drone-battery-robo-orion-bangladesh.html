The 2000mAh 3.7V Lithium Polymer (Li-Po) Drone Battery is the ultimate upgrade for hobbyists seeking longer flight durations. Specifically designed for 1S (3.7V) power systems, this high-capacity battery provides a significant boost in operational time for large single-cell RC drones, quadcopters, and remote-controlled boats compared to standard stock batteries.

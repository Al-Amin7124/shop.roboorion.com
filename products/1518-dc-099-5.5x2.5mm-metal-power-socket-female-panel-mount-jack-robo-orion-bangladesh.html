When building high-end DIY electronics, custom audio amplifiers, or robust battery charging stations, standard plastic DC sockets often fail under physical stress or heat. The DC-099 5.5x2.5mm Metal Power Socket is the industry-standard upgrade. Designed with a 5.5mm outer diameter and a slightly thicker 2.5mm inner pin, this premium female DC jack is specifically engineered to handle high-current power adapters cleanly and safely.

What sets the DC-099 apart is its incredibly durable, all-metal threaded casing. Complete with a secure mounting hex nut, this socket fastens rock-solid onto the panels of 3D-printed project boxes, aluminum chassis, or wooden enclosures. It will not strip, crack, or push inward, no matter how many times you forcefully insert or remove the power plug.

For maximum versatility, the DC-099 features three highly conductive solder lug terminals (Tip, Sleeve, and Switch). The switch terminal is especially useful for battery-powered projects, allowing the circuit to automatically disconnect the internal battery supply the moment an external wall adapter is plugged into the charging base.

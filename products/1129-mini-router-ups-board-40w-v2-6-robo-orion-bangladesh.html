The Mini Router UPS Board 40W V2.6 is an internationally standardized, high-quality power module from a reliable Chinese brand. Designed to keep your internet connection flawless during power outages, the board’s primary feature ensures that your Wi-Fi router or ONU does not restart when power transitions between mains and battery.

With a capability to push up to 47.5W maximum output, this compact board is equipped with four dedicated output ports (12V, 9V, and 5V) making it ideal to power both a Router and an ONU simultaneously. The board is built entirely using SMD (Surface Mount Device) components to efficiently pack high-tier components into a very small footprint.

It features a robust protection protocol, including Thunder Protection, Short Circuit Protection, Output Overvoltage Protection, and Polarity Protection. Additionally, the on-board LED indicator easily informs you of the 3S Li-Po battery level at 25%, 50%, 75%, and 100%.

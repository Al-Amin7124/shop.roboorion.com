Designed for universal compatibility with classic Hakko 936, 8586, and 852D style soldering handles, this "Class A" premium replacement tip is a game-changer for serious electronics technicians and DIY hobbyists.

The 900M-T-3C features a distinct 3mm bevel or "horseshoe" edge shape. This unique angled geometry allows the tip to hold a small bead of molten solder on its flat face, making it incredibly popular for rapid "drag soldering" on fine-pitch surface mount (SMD) chips, as well as efficiently pre-tinning thicker stranded wires before joining.

What separates a "Class A" tip from generic replacements is the internal construction. It boasts a solid oxygen-free copper core for rapid heat recovery, coated in multiple layers of iron, nickel, and chromium to resist oxidation. Fully RoHS compliant and lead-free, it provides an environmentally protective, long-lasting surface that wets perfectly with solder.

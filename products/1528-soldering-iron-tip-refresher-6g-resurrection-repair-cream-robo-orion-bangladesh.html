Every electronics technician knows the frustration of a dead soldering tip. Over time, high heat causes the tip to oxidize, turning black and completely rejecting solder. The Soldering Iron Tip Refresher (6g) – Resurrection & Repair Cream is a chemical lifesaver. Instead of throwing away a perfectly good tip or ruining it with sandpaper, this "resurrection paste" chemically restores the surface to factory condition in seconds.

This highly effective repair cream is a specialized mixture of active flux and micro-fine tin powder. When you dip your hot, blackened iron into the paste, the flux aggressively boils away the stubborn oxidation layer, while the tin powder immediately melts to coat the freshly cleaned metal. The result is a shiny, perfectly tinned tip that holds solder beautifully, ensuring your PCB repairs and wire splices are conductive and strong.

Packaged in a convenient 6-gram tub, this tip tinner is highly economical. Because you only need a quick dip to restore a tip, a single container will last through months of heavy workbench use. It is a must-have maintenance tool for hobbyists, students, and professional mobile repair technicians across Bangladesh.

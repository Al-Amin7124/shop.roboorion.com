Robo Orion BD provides professional, high-quality Custom Battery Pack building services tailored specifically to your power requirements. Whether you are building an electric bike, a high-capacity solar inverter system, a remote IoT sensor node, or a 12V Router UPS, our expert team can construct the perfect lithium-based power source for your project.

We utilize only rigorous, A-Grade cells, allowing you to choose between energy-dense Lithium-Ion (Li-ion 18650 / 21700) or ultra-safe, long-cycle Lithium Iron Phosphate (LiFePO4 32700 / 32140) chemistries. Every pack is meticulously assembled using professional spot welders and 99.7% pure nickel strips to minimize internal resistance and prevent voltage drop under heavy loads.

Safety and durability are non-negotiable. Therefore, we incorporate appropriately rated Battery Management Systems (BMS) in every build to protect against overcharge, deep discharge, and short circuits. Positive terminals are insulated with Kapton or Barley paper, wired with heat-resistant silicone AWG cables, and tightly sealed in thick PVC battery wrapping poly. Get the exact voltage, capacity, and connector type you need, crafted reliably in Bangladesh.

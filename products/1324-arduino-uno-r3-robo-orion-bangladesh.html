The Arduino Uno R3 is an industry-standard, open-source microcontroller board based on the removable ATmega328P DIP chip, widely used for robotics projects, sensor interfacing, embedded systems, and STEM education in Bangladesh. Featuring 14 digital input/output pins (6 capable of PWM output), 6 analog input pins, a 16 MHz crystal oscillator, a USB Type-B connection, a DC power jack, and an ICSP header, it contains everything needed to support the microcontroller.

The DIP socket configuration is a key advantage for beginners and academic environments. Because the main ATmega328P chip rests in a removable socket, if you accidentally damage the microcontroller due to an overvoltage spike or short circuit, you can replace the DIP chip for a small cost without having to discard the entire development board. The board can be powered directly via a USB connection or through an external DC power supply ranging from 7V to 12V DC.

Fully supported by the cross-platform Arduino IDE, the Uno R3 allows users to upload code with a single click. Thanks to its standardized shield pinout, it works with hundreds of plug-in expansion shields, including motor drivers, LCD displays, Ethernet interfaces, and wireless communication modules. The Arduino Uno R3 is a reliable choice for Bangladeshi university engineering students, school STEM programs, and prototyping makers.

Features

  1. Removable DIP-28 Chip: Socketed ATmega328P chip allows straightforward chip replacement in case of accidental damage.

The DC 0-100V 10A Digital Voltmeter Ammeter is a compact, highly accurate panel meter designed for real-time monitoring of voltage and current in electronic circuits. It features a bright, dual 0.28-inch LED display, showing voltage in red and current in blue simultaneously, making it incredibly easy to read at a glance.

With a voltage measurement range from 0 to 100V and a current reading capacity up to 10A, this module comes with a built-in internal shunt, eliminating the need to purchase an external one. It operates on a separate power supply range of 4V to 30V DC and delivers precision readings with minimal error (±0.1% for voltage and ±1% for current).

Its snap-in panel-mount design makes it the perfect addition to custom benchtop power supplies, solar energy setups, battery charge controllers, and automotive electrical diagnostics.

This 12V 3 Inch Cooling Fan is a high-performance brushless DC fan designed for efficient heat dissipation in a variety of electronic environments. With dimensions of 80x80x25mm, this fan is perfectly sized for desktop computer cases, amplifiers, and various DIY robotics projects that require steady airflow to prevent overheating.

Engineered for quiet operation, it utilizes a high-quality sleeve bearing design and thermoplastic UL94V-0 PBT blades, ensuring a long life expectancy of up to 50,000 hours. The brushless motor minimizes friction and wear, resulting in a stable 3500 RPM speed and consistent, reliable airflow for thermal management.

One of the key advantages of this fan is its voltage flexibility; by adjusting the input voltage, you can easily control the fan speed to achieve a nearly silent effect for noise-sensitive applications like home theater cabinets, audio amplifiers, or medical equipment.

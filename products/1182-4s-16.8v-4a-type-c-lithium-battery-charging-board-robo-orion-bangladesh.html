The 4S 16.8V Type-C Lithium Battery Charging Board is a highly efficient boost-charging module engineered explicitly for 4-cell lithium-ion or lithium-polymer battery packs. Featuring a modern Type-C USB input, it steps up a standard 5V USB source to deliver a stable 16.8V output, providing convenient, universal power delivery for your high-voltage projects.

Built for speed and safety, this specific version handles up to 4A of input current, translating to faster, optimized charging for your 4S packs. It operates at a 1MHz switching frequency for excellent energy conversion and comes fully equipped with smart battery management functions. These include overvoltage protection, low-input safeguards, and short-circuit prevention. Additionally, its quasi-constant voltage mode is designed to compensate for internal battery resistance, maximizing battery lifespan and charge efficiency.

Two onboard LED indicators (CR for charging, OK for full) offer real-time status updates. Ideal for developing custom high-power drone batteries, heavy-duty DIY power banks, or upgrading legacy 4S devices, this board ensures professional-grade power management in a miniature form factor.

# Product Image Editing Instructions (Robo Orion Standard)

## 🎯 Objective
Edit the provided product image for e-commerce product card usage with a clean, professional, and consistent visual style.

---

## 🧱 Canvas Settings
- Aspect Ratio: **4:3**
- Recommended Size: **1200 × 900 px** (or higher with same ratio)
- Alignment: **Center the product**

---

## 🎨 Background
- Type: **Solid or subtle gradient**
- Color: **Dark Gray**
  - Preferred: `#2F2F2F` to `#3A3A3A`
- Optional: Very subtle **radial gradient**
  - Center: slightly lighter gray  
  - Edges: darker gray  

---

## 📦 Product Handling
- **Do NOT alter the product itself**
- Maintain:
  - Original color
  - Original texture
  - Original proportions
- Clean background only (remove original background)

---

## 🔄 Orientation
- Default: Keep original orientation  
- If specified: rotate (e.g., **90° left/right**)  

---

## 🌫️ Shadow
- Add **soft drop shadow**
  - Low opacity
  - Slight blur
  - Centered under product
- Purpose: Create depth (not distraction)

---

## 💧 Watermark (Robo Orion Style)
- Text: **"Robo Orion"**
- Style:
  - Outline only (no fill / transparent inside)
  - Thin stroke
  - Low opacity
- Placement:
  - Repeated pattern across background
  - Even spacing
  - Should NOT distract from product

---

## ✨ Enhancement
- Slight adjustments:
  - Contrast: +5–10%
  - Sharpness: Slight increase
- Avoid over-editing

---

## 🚫 Avoid
- Pure black background (#000000)
- Bright or colorful backgrounds
- Heavy shadows
- Large or distracting watermark
- Changing product shape or color

---

## ✅ Output Style
- Clean
- Minimal
- Premium e-commerce look
- Suitable for product cards / online store

---

## 📌 Usage
In a new chat:
1. Upload product image  
2. Paste this file  
3. Add any extra instruction (e.g., rotate, zoom, etc.)
